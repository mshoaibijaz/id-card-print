          Layout & Print

          Image Upload

          • One card: click the Front and Back boxes, or drop an image on each. The back is optional.
          • Several cards: click “Choose images” and select all images at once: for each card the front, then the back. Check the pairs in the window that opens.
          • Each image: the icons above it open it in the Photo Editor, replace it or remove it; ⇄ swaps front and back.
          • Next step: the bar under the images says what to do next, with its button. Images of 300 DPI or more print sharp.
          2

          Layout & Print

          Card Size and Copies

          • Size: the width and height of the finished card. “Standard ID size” sets 85.6 × 54 mm; the chain keeps the proportions.
          • Unit: millimetres or inches, for every size in the app.
          • Image fit: when the image proportions differ from the card: stretch it, fill the card (edges cut off) or fit the whole image (white edges).
          • Copies: how many to print. “Fill one page” uses every place on the page; the line below the field says what one copy gives.
          3

          Layout & Print

          Layout Options

          • Side-by-side pairs: each front sits next to its back on the same page; print on one side of the paper.
          • Separate pages: all fronts on one page and the backs on the reverse side, for real double-sided cards.
          • Sides: one-sided or double-sided. Double-sided: the printer turns the paper itself (automatic duplex), or you print the fronts, put the paper back and print the backs (manual).
          • Flip on: choose the same edge as in the print dialog. After a test print, a small shift lines the backs up exactly.
          4

          Layout & Print

          Paper, Margins and Spacing

          • Paper: the size in your printer (A4, Letter … or a custom size), portrait or landscape.
          • Distribute evenly: fits as many cards as possible, with equal margins and gaps. Turn it off to type your own values.
          • Margins and spacing: blank space at the paper edges and between the cards, in mm or inches.
          • Cutting: dotted cutting guides and thin card outlines make cutting easier; “Center the cards” keeps them in the middle.

          Layout & Print

          Print Preview

          • Exactly as printed: Front / Back shows both sides of the page, the arrows show the other pages.
          • Zoom: starts at “Fit to window”. The mouse wheel zooms at the pointer; drag the page to move around; 100% is the actual size.
          • Print: in the print dialog choose the same paper, scale 100% (Actual size) and turn off “Fit to page”. The settings icon next to Print explains it.
          • Download: save the layout as a PDF or a picture (PNG, JPEG) to print later or at a print shop.

          Photo Editor

          Photos

          • Load photos: the front and back of the card, from a phone or a camera (JPG, PNG, WebP), or drop them here.
          • Front / Back: click a box to edit that photo; ⇄ swaps them, × removes one.
          • Several cards: the arrows above switch between the cards; “Use all” puts every card into the layout at once.
          • Status: under each photo you see whether it is already used in the layout.

          Photo Editor

          Crop

          • Detect card: the card’s 4 corners are found by themselves when a photo is opened.
          • 4-corner: drag the points onto the card corners; a magnifier helps. The card is straightened, even in a photo taken at an angle.
          • Rectangular: a straight frame, for photos taken from straight above. Proportions: ID card, the photo’s own, or free.
          • Use Front / Use Back: puts the cropped card into the layout. Zoom with the mouse wheel or the zoom box.

          Photo Editor

          Rotate

          • Rotate: turn the photo left or right by 90°.
          • Flip: mirror the photo horizontally or vertically, for example a mirrored selfie.
          • Straighten: the slider fixes a small tilt.
          • Check: the Result box shows the card as it will be printed.

          Photo Editor

          Adjust

          • Auto enhance: evens out dull or dark lighting and colour casts in one click.
          • Sliders: brightness, contrast, saturation and sharpness. Too much sharpness adds noise.
          • Black & white: for a print without colour.
          • Hold: before: hold the button to compare with the photo before the changes. Reset sets everything back.

          Photo Editor

          Result and Output

          • Result: the finished card, exactly as it goes into the layout.
          • Quality: 600 DPI is recommended, 300 DPI is good; higher values make larger files.
          • Rounded corners: cut the corners round like a real card (standard ID cards: 3.18 mm).
          • Both photos: these settings apply to the front and the back.
